Relationships

Purpose

This page provides a plain-language summary of relationship topics for moderation context. It contains no links, advertisements, or external content.

Key Concepts

  • Consent and mutual respect are foundational to healthy relationships.
  • Communication and honesty build trust between partners.
  • Boundaries should be discussed openly and honored.
  • Conflict is normal and can be resolved constructively.
  • Support networks and professional help are available when needed.

Moderation Guidelines

  • Content should be respectful and free of harassment.
  • No content promoting coercion, manipulation, or harm.
  • Age-appropriate standards must be maintained.
  • Personal information should never be shared without consent.

Scope
